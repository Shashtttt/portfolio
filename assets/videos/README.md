# Video Walkthroughs and Screen Recordings

Drop your `.mp4` or `.webm` dashboard screen recordings directly into this folder!

### Recommended formats:
1. **Short looping clip (10–15s):** Showing slicers/filters moving.
   - Recommended filename: `sales-demo.mp4`, `churn-demo.mp4`
2. **Full walkthrough video (1–2 minutes):** Explaining the project and insights.
   - You can also upload your video to [Loom](https://www.loom.com/) or [YouTube](https://youtube.com/) and paste the link directly into `js/projects-data.js`!
