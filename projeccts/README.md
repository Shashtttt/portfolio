# Data Analysis Projects

This repository showcases a collection of data analysis projects, interactive Power BI dashboards, and analytical presentation reports.

## 📊 Projects Overview

### 1. Power BI Dashboards (`.pbix`)
- **Goods Consumption Analysis (`goods consumption.pbix`)**: Visualizing consumer goods consumption metrics, volume distribution, and consumption trends.
- **Property & Real Estate Market Analysis (`Property.pbix`)**: Interactive dashboard evaluating property prices, location patterns, and real estate market dynamics.
- **Tata Sales Performance (`Tata Sales.pbix`)**: Sales analytics, revenue breakdown, and growth metrics for Tata products.
- **Sales & Business Analytics Dashboard (`shashvat.pbix`)**: End-to-end business intelligence dashboard monitoring key business KPIs.

### 2. Analytical Reports & Presentations (`.pdf`)
- **Customer Churn Analysis (`Churn.pdf`)**: Analysis of customer attrition, key churn factors, retention strategies, and cohort patterns.
- **Netflix Data Analysis (`netflix.pdf`)**: Visual storytelling and insights on content library trends, genre popularity, ratings, and global releases.
- **Second-Hand Vehicle Sales Insights (`Second hand Vehicle_sales.pdf`)**: Market evaluation of pre-owned car sales, pricing drivers, mileage vs. valuation, and brand trends.

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies
- **Business Intelligence**: Microsoft Power BI
- **Reporting & Visualization**: Data Storytelling, KPI Scorecards, Executive Summaries
- **Analysis Types**: Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA), Sales Analytics, Churn Prediction & Retention
